## Features Overview

### Deep Foundations
- **LCPC Method (Bustamante & Gianeselli)**: CPT-based axial pile capacity.
- **Koppejan Method**: Base and shaft resistance calculation with NaN-safe window filtering.
- **De Beer Method**: Eurocode 7 compliant pile resistance from CPT data.
- **Axial Capacity (AxCap)**: Compression & tension capacity curves.
- **Negative Skin Friction**: Zeevaert & De Beer single/group pile calculations.
and much more ...

### Eurocode 7
- Parameter selection (Constant values & linear trend analysis).
- Partial factors calculator for STR/GEO limit states.

### AGS Converter
- Native parser for AGS v3.1 & v4 site investigation files.
