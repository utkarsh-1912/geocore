Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Is GeoCore truly free and open source?

Yes! GeoCore is released under the GNU General Public License v2 (GPL v2). You can use it in commercial engineering design projects, academic research, and consulting without any licensing fees or seat restrictions.

Does GeoCore send project soil data to the cloud?

No. GeoCore is 100% offline-first. All computations, CPT interpretations, and AGS conversions run purely locally on your machine over a local loopback port (`127.0.0.1:8000`). Your subsurface project data never leaves your computer.

Can I import standard AGS 4.0 files directly?

Yes. GeoCore has a built-in AGS 4.0 parser that extracts all standard groups (PROJ, HOLE, SAMP, ISPT, GEOL, etc.) with automatic unit checking, coordinate mapping, and one-click conversion to SoilProfile layers.

How are calculation results verified?

All algorithms in GeoCore are unit-tested against peer-reviewed benchmark datasets, textbook solutions, and standard published papers (e.g. Robertson 1990/2016, Bustamante & Gianeselli 1982, Koppejan 1948, Vesic 1973, Schneider 1997).
